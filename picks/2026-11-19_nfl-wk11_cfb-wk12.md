### What this tells us

1. **The 70–79 band isn't finding an edge.** At 41% over 29 games, it loses money at any Underdog multiplier. Its *upper* half (74–79) is worse, at 3–9. Those games mostly reached 74+ on expert consensus (B/R panels, writer picks). Writer consensus looks like a public signal, not an edge, and the scoring has been rewarding it.
2. **QB downgrades that the line already reflects aren't an edge.** Duke -13 vs Clemson's third-string QB lost outright. The line had already moved 2 points and the total had dropped 4. Steelers -4.5 vs the Eagles' backup QB lost outright too. When the market has already moved on an injury, don't add points for it.
3. **Sharp-money splits are 4–3 over two weeks** (W: Tennessee, Duke last week, Iowa, Michigan RLM; L: Duke this week, Baylor, TCU). That's no better than a coin flip so far. Stop adding much weight for them.
4. **Late-night road favorites are now 1–2** (Utah covered at TCU). Not a reliable fade.
5. **Big favorites on the board went 4–2 laying 10+.** Wake and Notre Dame both covered by half a point, so that record is lucky, not a skill signal.
6. **The one Great bet (Wake +1.5) hit by 42.** Its setup was a line that moved on an injury, then the player was cleared, with models still showing a 4–5 point gap. That's the only pattern with a clean result. With n=1 it's a hypothesis, not proof.

### Changes for next week

- **Bet only 80+. If nothing reaches 80, don't bet.** This was already advised, and two weeks of data back it.
- **Cut writer and expert picks to at most 5 of the 20 agreement points.** Let independent models (numberFire, BlueChip, Dimers, SportsLine sims) and market prices (Kalshi) carry the score.
- **No personnel bonus for injuries the line has already absorbed.** Add points only when the line hasn't moved yet, or moved the wrong way relative to the news.
- **Keep grading the No-go tier.** If it keeps landing near 50%, the 0–100 score is mostly noise below 80, and the board should be cut down to the few games that clear 80.
- **Monday:** Bengals -5.5 is a 70 (Good). Under the rules above, it's a log-only game, not a bet.
