### Why each slot pick

- **Colts +12 (TNF):** Houston is 0–4 ATS at home and 2–5 as a favorite. Indianapolis won the first meeting 19–17. The line moved from 10.5 to 12–13, and only 6% of moneyline bets are on the Colts but they account for 36% of the money (sharp). Covers, DocSports, GameHaus and SI all took the Colts, while the B/R panel went 5–2 Texans. The line ranges from 9.5 to 13 depending on the book; take +12 or better.
- **Duke -13 (Fri):** Clemson starts its third-string QB, Trent Pearman, a former walk-on making his first career start. Starter Reynolds has a torn meniscus, and backup Vizzina is out with a concussion. Duke -13.5 has 85% of the money on 41% of bets (sharp). Predictem projects Duke by 17. Dimers projects 14–15, roughly the line. The total dropped from 44.5 to 40.5. This is a double-digit spread, so don't lay more than 14.
- **Wake Forest -20.5:** numberFire projects Wake by about 27 (a 6.7-point edge). Wake beat SMU 49–7 last week. But it's a 20-point road favorite with a single model behind it, so it's a weak slot.
- **Baylor +3.5:** Baylor has 56% of the money on 21% of bets (sharp). ESPN's Maldonado took Baylor and likes the moneyline. numberFire projects only about 1.7 for Texas Tech. B/R's Kenyon has Texas Tech by 13.
- **Notre Dame -20.5:** numberFire projects Notre Dame by about 25 and B/R by 25. Two sources, but a big spread.
- **Steelers -4.5:** The Eagles start backup QB Joe Milton again. B/R's panel went 6–1 Steelers, and GameHaus took Pittsburgh. Covers took Philadelphia, citing the low total (37.5), which makes points valuable. The Steelers just lost 38–18, so the line may be soft.
- **Bengals -5.5 (MNF):** Burrow and Cincinnati have scored 31+ in five straight games. B/R went 5–2, and Covers and GameHaus took Cincinnati. The line moved from 4.5 to 6.5 at some books, so take 5.5 or better.

### Lessons carried forward
- Only Great bets have earned money so far. Good-tier games are 6–9.
- Recheck every starting QB on game day, college included.
- Confirm kickoff day and time from ESPN or the school's site.

- Weight sharp-money splits more.
- Watch late-night road favorites (0–2) and double-digit spreads (2–2).
