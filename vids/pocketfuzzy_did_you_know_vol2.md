# Pocketfuzzy — "Did You Know?" Animation Pack (Vol. 2)

25 short animated explainers, each tied to something that went viral between late October and late November 2026. Numbering continues from Vol. 1 (episodes 13–37). Every episode is built to be **fully original cartoon**: the trend lives in the voiceover and caption only, never in the picture.

---

## 0. Global settings

Everything from **Vol. 1, Section 0** applies unchanged: 9:16 at 1080×1920, 20–30 s (flexible), 24 fps, 4–5 shots, burned-in word-by-word captions centered at y ≈ 1360, the bottom-left `pocketfuzzy` watermark at 70%, the armless Fuzz, voice ducking, and the series bookends. Music now follows the **Music rotation** below rather than the old music-box bed.

### Style block — paste at the end of EVERY visual prompt

> **STYLE:** hand-drawn 2D animation, slightly scratchy ink linework, muted mossy greens, damp browns and dusty lavender, warm lamplight glow, soft film grain, cozy-but-grimy storybook feel, small creatures with big expressive eyes, shallow depth of field, no text in frame, no logos, no real people's likenesses.

### Original-art rules (new in Vol. 2)

These episodes ride real news, so every episode has a **Never draw** line. The general rules:

1. **No real people.** Celebrities, athletes and executives are never drawn, even "inspired by." Use generic characters or no people at all.
2. **No other studios' characters or worlds.** No film/game/show characters, creatures, symbols, title cards, color schemes or signature looks.
3. **No logos or brand liveries.** Brand names can be *said* in VO when they're part of the fact; logos, packaging and store/airline/team designs are never drawn.
4. **No recreations of viral images.** Don't redraw the meme or the news photo. Draw the underlying subject your own way.
5. **Sensitive news gets a gentle tone.** Ep 13 (hippo) and Ep 23 (quokka) are about real animals that were harmed. No jokes at their expense; both end on a kindness/PSA card.
6. **Don't put trend names in generator prompts.** Words like "Godzilla," "Pokémon" or "James Bond" make video generators copy the IP. Prompts describe only what's on screen.

**Length:** ~22s · **Voice:** Heart · **Trend:** Costco water scramble / warehouse-store memes
**Hook:** One price hasn't moved in over forty years.

### Voiceover script
> One price hasn't moved in over forty years.
> Costco's hot dog and drink combo cost a dollar fifty in 1985, and it still costs a dollar fifty today.
> If it had kept up with inflation, it would be well over four dollars.
> To protect it, the company even started making its own hot dogs.
> It's not a meal anymore. It's a promise.
